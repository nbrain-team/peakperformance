Episode Overview

In this episode of Peak Property Performance, Bill Douglas and Drew Hall sit down with Michael Mullen, a seasoned expert in property management software, to unpack the complexities of technology sprawl within the commercial real estate (CRE) sector. Michael shares his extensive experience, tracing the evolution of CRE systems from operator-controlled environments to platform-driven ecosystems, highlighting the challenges that arise when complexity outweighs clarity.

We get into what actually breaks in the real world, what Michael learned the hard way, and what operators can implement to create more efficient and controlled environments. The discussion covers the pitfalls of losing visibility and control due to centralized systems, and how operators can reclaim control by driving vendor roadmaps rather than passively accepting them.

“The operator is paying for a system, but the system actually owns the operator because modern platforms often trade control for convenience.”

— Michael Mullen
