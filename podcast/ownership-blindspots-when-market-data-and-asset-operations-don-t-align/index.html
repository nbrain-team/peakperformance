Ownership Blindspots: When Market Data and Asset Operations Don’t Align

Episode 29 · 26 min

Listen onSpotifyApple PodcastsYouTubeiHeartRSS Feed

Welcome back to Peak Property Performance! In today's episode, we're digging deep into the critical gap between market insights and actual asset performance in commercial real estate. Most owners rely on reports and conferences to gauge the market, but as our guest Rick Lackey reveals, the real truth is found in the deals themselves — where buyers' actions, real pricing, and operational realities play out.

We'll explore why so many CRE assets look perfect on a spreadsheet but break down in real world operations, uncover the most common information disconnects between brokers, owners, and operators, and discuss how top performers bridge the gap between insight and execution. Along the way, we'll learn why actively listening to your property — and the people inside it — is essential, and how data-driven operations are transforming the industry.

If you're ready to understand where CRE deals succeed or fail, and how to turn market knowledge into peak property performance, this episode is for you!
