Leveraging Operational Data to Improve Lender Confidence in Commercial Real Estate

Episode 23 · 36 min

Listen onSpotifyApple PodcastsYouTubeiHeartRSS Feed

Welcome to this episode of Peak Property Performance, where host Drew Hall sits down with Dr. Thomas Schneider, founder and CEO of Finloop, to explore how data and digital strategies are reshaping the landscape of commercial real estate debt. Together, they dive into the ways operational data—from maintenance histories to system performance—can influence lender confidence, streamline loan reporting, and ultimately help owners secure more efficient, competitively priced financing.

Dr. Thomas Schneider brings a unique global perspective, addressing the differences between European and US markets, the evolution toward transparency, and the rise of integrated PropTech and FinTech solutions. The conversation unpacks the transition from siloed, vendor-owned dashboards to holistic, multi-system data collection, revealing how AI and digital maturity are driving smarter underwriting and reducing financing friction.

Whether you're a seasoned real estate professional or just curious about how technology is making debt markets more accessible, this episode offers actionable insights on preparing properties for financing, navigating industry trends, and leveraging clean, comprehensive data for better outcomes. Plus, stick around for personal insights from Dr. Thomas Schneider, as he shares career advice, daily habits, and what keeps him inspired in both work and life.
