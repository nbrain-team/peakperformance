Cookie Policy

Last updated: May 27, 2026

This Cookie Policy explains what cookies are, what types of cookies are placed on your device when you visit peakpropertyperformance.com (the "Site"), and how we use them.

What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your device (computer, tablet, or mobile) when you visit a website. They help the site remember your preferences and understand how you interact with it. Cookies may be "first-party" (set by the site you're visiting) or "third-party" (set by a different domain).

How We Use Cookies

We categorize our cookies into three groups. You can manage your preferences at any time by clicking "Cookie Settings" in the site footer or using the consent banner that appears on your first visit.

Marketing

These cookies enable personalized advertising and remarketing across platforms. We do not currently deploy any marketing cookies. This category exists to future-proof our consent framework. If marketing cookies are introduced, this policy will be updated and your prior consent will not carry over — you will be prompted again.

Google Consent Mode v2

We use Google Consent Mode v2. This means:

  • When Analytics cookies are denied, Google Analytics still loads but sends only cookieless, aggregate "pings" — no cookies are set, no personal identifiers are stored, and no individual tracking occurs.
  • When Analytics cookies are granted, full measurement (including the _ga cookies listed above) is activated.

This approach lets us preserve basic aggregate traffic counts while respecting your choice.

How to Control Cookies

On this Site

  • Consent banner: Appears on your first visit if you are in the EU/EEA/UK. Choose "Accept All," "Reject All," or "Manage Preferences" to set category-level preferences.
  • Cookie Settings link: Available in the footer of every page. Click it at any time to revisit and change your preferences.

In your browser

Most browsers allow you to block or delete cookies via their settings. Note that blocking all cookies may affect Site functionality. For details:

  • Google Chrome
  • Mozilla Firefox
  • Safari
  • Microsoft Edge

Google Analytics opt-out

You can also install the Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on to prevent Google Analytics from collecting data across all sites.

Consent Expiry and Re-Consent

Your consent preferences expire after 13 months, in accordance with GDPR guidance. After expiry, you will be prompted again. You may also revoke consent at any time by clicking "Cookie Settings" in the footer.

International Visitors

If you are located in the European Union, European Economic Area, or the United Kingdom, non-essential cookies (Analytics and Marketing) are denied by default. They will only be activated after you provide explicit, affirmative consent via the cookie banner.

If you are located outside of these regions, all cookie categories are auto-enabled. You may still adjust your preferences using the "Cookie Settings" link.

Changes to This Policy

We will update this Cookie Policy when our cookie usage changes. The "Last updated" date at the top reflects the most recent revision.
