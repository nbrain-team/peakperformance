Be on the Show

Pitch yourself as a guest on the PPP Podcast.

CRE operators, PropTech founders, capital allocators, and category experts — if you have something sharp to say about AI, data, or digital infrastructure in commercial real estate, we want to hear it.

Who fits

Who we book

Operators with real war stories, founders shipping real product, capital allocators with a clear lens on the cycle, and category experts who can teach owners something they didn't already know. We pass on guests who only have a deck.

What to expect

What being a guest looks like

Roughly 45–60 minutes of recording, lightly edited. Co-hosts Bill Douglas and Drew Hall (or one of them solo). Camera optional but encouraged for video clips.

We align on topic, key points, and audience takeaways. No surprise questions on tape.

No PR fluff. We push for specifics — what worked, what failed, what you'd do differently.
